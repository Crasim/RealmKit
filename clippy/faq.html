Answers

  • How do refunds work?

    Demo refunds are approved within two business days. Once approved, the original payment method is credited automatically and a confirmation email is sent to the billing address on file.

    Keywords: refund, billing, repayment, money back

    Anchor link

  • Can I export my project data?

    Yes. Open the project menu, choose Export, and select either JSON for raw data or CSV for a spreadsheet-friendly export. Larger exports are prepared in the background and delivered by email.

    Keywords: export, CSV, JSON, download

    Anchor link

  • Why am I seeing permission prompts?

    Permission prompts appear whenever the assistant wants to read the page, interact with the UI, modify content, create elements, remove elements, or run stronger capabilities like JavaScript. You can tune those categories from the settings panel.

    Keywords: permission, interact, modify, settings

    Anchor link

  • Does the widget work on mobile?

    Yes. A normal tap opens the widget, while a drag gesture moves it only after the touch passes the drag threshold. This page is also useful for checking that the character does not drift or snap while idle on smaller screens.

    Keywords: mobile, tap, drag, touch

    Anchor link
