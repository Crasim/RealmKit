About this demo

Click the paperclip in the bottom-right corner to open Clippy. Clippy is connected to a local Ollama model and can read and change anything on this page. All actions that touch the page are gated by per-capability permissions — you decide what Clippy may do.

Try asking things like:
• "Change the page heading to 'Hello, Jimmy!'"
• "Add three more items to the task list."
• "Make the counter reach 42."
• "Switch the page to dark mode."
• "Fill in the contact form with plausible fake values, then summarise what you put in each field."
• "Go to the FAQ page, find the refund answer, and scroll me to it."
